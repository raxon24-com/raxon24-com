## 🌍 About Raxon24.com

**Raxon24.com** is a financial trading platform focused on providing a streamlined environment for accessing global markets. The platform is designed around **security, speed, accessibility, market information, and advanced trading tools**, helping traders navigate different financial instruments from a single environment.

Raxon24 presents an all-in-one trading experience covering multiple asset classes and provides tools intended to support informed trading decisions.

> **Risk Warning:** Trading CFDs and leveraged financial products involves a high level of risk and may result in rapid loss of capital. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Users should understand the risks and consider their financial circumstances before trading.

## 🔄 Withdrawals

According to the published withdrawal policy:

1. Complete required identity and address verification.
2. Submit a withdrawal request through the applicable account channel.
3. Requests are generally described as being processed within **5 business days**, although timing may vary.
4. Withdrawals are generally restricted to accounts held in the same name as the trading account.
5. Additional documentation may be requested for AML/KYC purposes or larger transactions.

### ⚠️ Important Withdrawal Policy Notice

The published policy also states that certain fees may apply when withdrawing more than 20% of profits or closing an account, and that such fees may be requested in advance.

## ⚠️ Risk Disclosure

Financial-market trading involves substantial risk.

Key risks described by Raxon24 include:

- **Market risk** — prices can move rapidly and unpredictably.
- **Leverage risk** — leverage can magnify both profits and losses.
- **Liquidity risk** — reduced liquidity can affect execution and pricing.
- **Political & economic risk** — geopolitical and macroeconomic events can create sudden volatility.
- **Technology risk** — outages, connectivity problems, technical errors, or inaccurate data can affect trading.
- **Regulatory risk** — laws and trading conditions can vary by jurisdiction.
- **Counterparty risk** — financial counterparties may face financial or operational difficulties.

Raxon24.com's website includes a warning that **75% of retail investor accounts experience financial losses when trading CFDs**. This statistic should be understood as a risk disclosure rather than a prediction of any individual trader's outcome.
